test(runtime): enforce Bun-only delivery job launch manifests and process identity (#1170, ADR-0002). New `tests/bun-only-launch-manifests.test.ts` (scanner in `tests/support/bun-only-launch-scan.ts`, child-process probe in `tests/support/bun-identity-probe.ts`) statically scans every job/worker LAUNCH path — discovered, not listed: `Dockerfile*` (FROM/RUN/CMD/ENTRYPOINT/HEALTHCHECK), `*.crontab` (including the `$AWCMS_RUN` launcher variable), `ops/run-job.sh` and other `*.sh`, `docker-compose*.yml`/`compose*.yml` services, `*.service` units, shebangs and `.github/workflows` — and fails on any Node.js launcher (`node`, `npm`, `npx`, `pnpm`, `yarn`, `tsx`, `ts-node`, ...) as a command, path, image or shebang. Every `oven/bun` image and `setup-bun` version must equal the `packageManager` pin read from `package.json` (CI's declared `engines.bun` floor cell excepted). Every scheduled and module-registry job target must resolve to a `package.json` script that is Bun end to end. Process identity: each guarded worker entrypoint (email, WhatsApp, push, Telegram, domain-events and every other `import.meta.main` job script) is imported in a child process with a scrubbed env and must report `process.versions.bun`, the `Bun` global and a Bun `process.execPath`. Positive and negative fixtures cover Bun `node:*` built-ins, `@astrojs/node`, `node_modules`, comments and prose (allowed) against Node images, shebangs, compose commands and systemd units (rejected). No runtime or manifest change; no migration, no `bun.lock` edit. A deployed-container smoke test needs a Docker daemon and stays out of scope.
