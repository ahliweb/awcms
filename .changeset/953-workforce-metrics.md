feat(hr_workforce): workforce metrics as transparent business facts (#953). Two read routes under `/api/v1/hr-workforce/workforce-metrics/`: `employees` (attendance, hours, shift coverage and a worked-to-scheduled productivity ratio per employee) and `groups` (per-office aggregates). Visibility is default-deny: an employee reads their own (`?mine=true`) and a supervisor their DIRECT reports (`?reports=true`) through ownership grants, HR needs the new `hr_workforce.workforce_metrics.read`, and the aggregate needs `workforce_metrics.analyze` and returns no individual row; another employee's id is 403, the same as a missing one. The aggregate takes only one whole closed UTC ISO week (sliding windows would isolate a person), counts as a contributor only an employee with at least four hours in the bucket, reports a contributor band instead of a count, and a group with fewer than five contributors returns no number at all; there is no tenant-wide total to subtract from. A supervisor sees a report only from when the reporting line began. No composite score, ranking or flag; no photo, biometric, device, location or payroll data is read; every response carries the governance notice (a figure needs the review in `docs/awcms/workforce-metrics.md` before any consequential use). The module registers the `hr_workforce.attendance_events` reporting projection (event volume per kind, HR-only). Migration `308` seeds the two permissions (not granted to any role; existing tenants need `identity-access:permissions:backfill`) and indexes the projection cursor. Docs EN and ID: `docs/awcms/workforce-metrics.md`.
