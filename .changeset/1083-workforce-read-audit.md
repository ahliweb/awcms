fix(hr_workforce): audit HR bulk reads of person-level workforce facts and stop naming suppressed offices (#1083). An all-employee read of the workforce-metrics employee list, the aggregate route, and the all-employee attendance list now leaves a coalesced `sensitive_read.workforce_metrics` / `sensitive_read.attendance_events` audit row (employment ids and a count only); `hr_workforce` joins `SENSITIVE_READ_MODULE_KEYS`, its other read permissions are classified with reasons, and the sensitive-read gate now reads the `authorize: [A, B]` array form. A suppressed aggregate group no longer carries `officeId` and is listed after the shown groups. The permission-combining risk (M1) is documented in `docs/awcms/workforce-metrics.md` (not an SoD rule: the owner role holds every permission). Follow-up from the security audit: the manager-chain `reports` mode is recorded too (attribute `mode`), attendance corrections (list, and the single record when its reason comes from the role grant) are a sensitive read (`sensitive_read.attendance_corrections`), the aggregate records the permission that actually granted (`.analyze` under `sensitive_read.workforce_metrics_aggregate`), the location-evidence audit row carries the employment ids, and the gate parser handles inline guard objects inside `authorize: [...]`. No migration.
