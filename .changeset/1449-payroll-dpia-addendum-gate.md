feat(privacy): payroll DPIA-addendum go-live gate, addendum and processing-terms templates (Issue #1449, DPIA residuals R-11 and R-13). The `hr_payroll` module (not built yet; #957 is blocked on the DPIA) cannot be enabled for a tenant (`409 PAYROLL_DPIA_ADDENDUM_REQUIRED` from `enableTenantModule`, which also covers presets), and every guarded endpoint of it is refused at the `authorizeInTransaction` chokepoint (`403 PAYROLL_DPIA_ADDENDUM_REQUIRED`, before any permission is looked up, independent of `awcms_tenant_modules` where "no row" means enabled), until the deployment sets `HR_PAYROLL_DPIA_ADDENDUM_REF`, `_APPROVED_ON` and `_SHA256` (all unset is the default and fails closed). `config:validate` rejects a half-set group and, when well formed, verifies the artifact `docs/awcms/dpia-addenda/<ref>.md` (digest, `status: approved`, signatory, counsel review date, O5 outcome A or B, every required section, no unfilled marker); `security:readiness` adds the critical check "Payroll DPIA addendum is on record before payroll is enabled" (artifact plus no enabled gated tenant row). New: the per-deployment addendum template covering both outcomes of owner decision O5 (which is NOT decided here) with operator-staff access and the K13 sign-off block, and a controller/operator processing-terms template (en + id). Legal references the author could not confirm are marked for counsel. No migration; no API shape change beyond the new refusal code.
