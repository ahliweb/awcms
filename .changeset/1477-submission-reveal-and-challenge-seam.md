feat(website_engagement): submission reveal screen and challenge-seam intake (Issue #1477 part 2). The submission detail screen (`/admin/website-engagement/{id}`) now has an explicit, confirmed "Reveal contact details" control, shown only to a holder of `website_engagement.submissions.reveal` for a `received` contact or lead; it POSTs once to the existing audited reveal endpoint (audit at warning, never the values) and renders the response as text in one panel emptied on Hide, after a minute and on `pagehide`. A refusal for lack of permission and for an unknown or foreign submission read as one sentence. `submissions.reveal` leaves the `NOT_YET_SCREENED` ledger. The public intake now verifies its challenge through the pre-session challenge seam (`enforcePublicFormCaptcha`: single-use burn, deadline, one provider, uniform refusal) instead of calling Turnstile itself, and its exception is removed from the `extension-points:check` allow-list; the per-source `challengeMode` and the per-request Origin hostname are kept (new optional `expectedHostname` on the seam). No migration, no API shape change. Rotation of a source token still has NO grace window (fail closed); whether to add one is an open owner decision recorded in the module README.
