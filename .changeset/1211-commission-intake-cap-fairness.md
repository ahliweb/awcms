Bound and harden the `hr_commission` intake queue (Issue #1211, follow-up to the #1189 audit, finding M2, L4 and L7; ADR-0147). A tenant may now hold at most 10,000 live (`pending` + `posting`) intake rows: the insert guard, a narrow `SECURITY DEFINER` trigger owned by the NOLOGIN `awcms_hr_commission_lifecycle_counter` role, answers the new `full` outcome (and `disabled` when the `hr_commission` module is off for the tenant) without raising inside the consumer's transaction, while `awcms_worker` still has no `SELECT` on the table. A replay of an existing row still answers `duplicate` at the cap. Claims are fair across employments and between `accrue` and `void` (accrue first within a turn), and the job drains tenants round-robin one batch per round, so one tenant's flood cannot starve another's rows. Idle tenants (switch off, no rows) cost no tenant transaction per tick, via the narrow `awcms_hr_commission_intake_non_idle_tenants()` function. The exceptions summary counts are bounded per status. Deferrals now have their own `deferrals` counter instead of spending the `attempts` retry budget (L7), and the dry-run retention count counts settled rows only (L4). Adds migration `sql/321`; no public API shape changes.
