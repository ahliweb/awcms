Record ADR-0147 (Issue #1138): an event-driven commission accrual, such as the commerce `order.paid` attributor that runs in `domain-events:dispatch` as `awcms_worker`, enters through an `hr_commission`-owned intake queue instead of calling `CommissionAccrualPort`. The worker role gets `INSERT` on that queue and nothing on any payroll-family object; a job that runs as `awcms_app` (which `ops/run-job.sh` already keeps `DATABASE_URL` for, Issue #1132) claims the intake rows and posts them through the one existing posting core, so the audit row, the domain event, the transition log and the segregation-of-duties snapshot are the synchronous path's own. Authorization is by descriptor registration plus a default-off per-tenant switch changed by a high-risk permission. `PAYROLL_ALLOWED_GRANTEES`, the empty `PAYROLL_WORKER_EXECUTE_ALLOWLIST` and `commission_accrual` 1.1.0 do not change; `SECURITY DEFINER` posting functions, an HTTP machine actor and wider worker grants are rejected with the analysis in the ADR. Documentation only (ADR with Indonesian mirror, ADR index, `hr-payroll.md` section 6.4 and the `hr_commission` README); no migration, route, job or port is added, and the downstream adapter stays dormant until the implementation issue lands.
