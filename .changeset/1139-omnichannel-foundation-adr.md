Accept ADR-0148 and its readiness pack (Issue #1139), the architecture admission for a tenant-safe multi-account omnichannel foundation. The foundation is five separately gated capabilities rather than one module: a new `channel_accounts` control plane (accounts, OAuth grants, per-account capability evidence, envelope-encrypted secret references, health), sender routing inside the one existing `whatsapp_delivery` queue (an immutable nullable `sender_account_id`, no queue per phone, no fallback, no number rotation), the planned `social_publishing` behind the existing port, an inbound event boundary whose store is left to #1144, and the CRM bridge kept downstream. Thirteen invariants bind every later ADR and implementation, among them fail-closed capability per account (a provider matrix with `candidate`, `restricted`, `not offered` and `unverified` cells), secrets only by reference, verified inbound or deferred, no cross-platform identity merge, and LinkedIn member data barred from CRM enrichment. The ADR narrowly amends the "credentials per deployment, never per tenant" rule of ADR-0074 and ADR-0133 for tenant-owned accounts, and records the owner's choice that a derived product may default to StarSender with Meta Cloud API as the alternate. The pack holds the logical data dictionary, the provider capability matrix v1, the threat model, the risk register, the contracts outline, the rollout and the test matrix, with English and Indonesian mirrors. Documentation only: no module, table, migration, route, AsyncAPI channel or connector is added, and nothing here is evidence that a connector works.
