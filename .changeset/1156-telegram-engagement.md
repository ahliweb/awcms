Add the `telegram_engagement` module: customer Telegram conversations through a tenant's own bots (Issue #1156, ADR-0152), a context separate from the staff-only `telegram_delivery`, which is unchanged and shares nothing with it (no import, table, queue, route, token or setting; the staff bot is refused as a customer bot). Many bots per tenant, each an existing `channel_accounts` account (provider `telegram`, kind `bot`) whose token is a write-only channel secret. One verified webhook per bot, `POST /api/v1/telegram-engagement/webhooks/{callbackToken}`: the tenant, bot and business scope come from the opaque per-bot callback token (stored only as a SHA-256), never from the update body, and the `X-Telegram-Bot-Api-Secret-Token` header (derived from an encrypted per-endpoint seed) is compared in constant time before the body is read; every failure is one generic `401`, audited in aggregate. Ingestion is idempotent on `(bot, update_id)`; private chats only, bot senders, groups, edited messages and forwarded `/start` change nothing. A contact exists only after `/start` (account-scoped keyed hash, chat id encrypted under the new `TELEGRAM_ENGAGEMENT_KEYS` ring, masked label); `/start` grants support only, marketing and transactional need an explicit opt-in button on a prompt this module sent (notice version recorded), and `/stop`, a block or a provider `403` withdraw every purpose at once; consent evidence is append-only. Replies and opted-in notices go through the module's own queue and `bun run telegram-engagement:dispatch` (connector: getMe identity binding, setWebhook with minimal `allowed_updates`, deleteWebhook on revoke; dispatcher: re-check at dispatch, per-bot and per-chat pacing, `429 retry_after` without spending budget, per-bot breaker and daily ceiling under `TELEGRAM_ENGAGEMENT_DAILY_CAP`, uncertain sends never retried). Events `awcms.telegram_engagement.interaction.received` (the ADR-0150 envelope: ids, kinds, privacy class; never a Telegram id, username or text) and `awcms.telegram_engagement.consent.changed`. Admin API for bots (register, rotate as a hard cut, revoke), masked contacts with per-purpose eligibility, the audited erase, and queueing a message or an opt-in prompt; RLS `FORCE` on all five tables, retention declared through data_lifecycle; migrations 350-353. The dispatcher connects only through the new `TELEGRAM_ENGAGEMENT_DATABASE_URL` as its own role `awcms_telegram_engagement_dispatch` (sql/353: column grants only, and a RESTRICTIVE policy that shows it nothing of the channel secrets but live customer bot tokens and webhook seeds; pinned by a new `security:readiness` check), with no fallback. The webhook route is exempted from the session-free body boundary, and `api:spec:check` now fails whenever a public body operation and that boundary disagree. Off by default (`TELEGRAM_ENGAGEMENT_ENABLED`), `experimental` with no admin screen yet; new permissions must be granted to a role before use.
