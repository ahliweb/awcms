security(channel_accounts): an external id is claimed by VERIFICATION where a connector can prove it, and a platform release action settles disputes (Issue #1215, external-id squatting; ADR-0170 amends ADR-0148 invariant I1 from "unique per live id" to "unique per claimed id"; owner decision K2, options A + C). `sql/416` adds `claim_basis` (`registration`, the fail-closed default, or `verification`; immutable by trigger), rebuilds `awcms_channel_accounts_live_external_key` as a partial unique index keyed `(provider, account_kind, external_id)` over the live rows that hold a claim (`registration`, or `connected`/`degraded`) and adds a per-tenant unique over every live row; the KIND is in the key and the shape of an id is validated per kind (`422 EXTERNAL_ID_INVALID`, the same for a held and a free id), so a Telegram channel or a Mailketing ESP login cannot squat a bot or a sender. Only `telegram` `bot` (the connector's `getMe` binds the token to the id) and `mailketing` `sender_identity` (the provider's own verified-sender list) claim by verification: a `pending` registration of those holds nothing against other tenants (two tenants can both register it, with no oracle), the first to become `connected` wins by the unique index (race-safe), and the loser stays `pending` with health `failing` / `ACCOUNT_ALREADY_REGISTERED` (the Telegram endpoint fails with the same code). Every other provider keeps the claim from registration, as before, and existing rows keep it too. New `POST /api/v1/channel-accounts/claims/release` (`{ provider, accountKind, externalId, reasonCode, ticketRef?, reason }`) is the dispute path: PLATFORM-scoped permission `channel_accounts.account_claims.revoke` (never seeded to tenant roles; a tenant owner gets the uniform `403`; it requires an ENROLLED MFA factor and a fresh step-up), `Idempotency-Key`, mandatory reason (no email or phone), one narrow SECURITY DEFINER read (`awcms_find_channel_account_claim_holder`, NOLOGIN owner `awcms_channel_claim_locator`) to find the holder, then the release runs in the holder tenant's own RLS context (credential ciphertext and webhook secrets erased, soft delete `CLAIM_RELEASED_BY_PLATFORM`, claim-less pending children ended with their container), audited `critical` in the platform trail (with the free text) and `warning` in the holder's trail (closed reason code and ticket reference only). The Telegram connector now claims the bot before `setWebhook`. `security:readiness` pins the column, both indexes and the locator exactly and probes the behaviour. The operator screen is not built (`NOT_YET_SCREENED`). Deployment note: the platform permission is granted to the setup tenant's owner by the migration and by nothing else (`identity-access:permissions:backfill` never grants a platform permission); when the platform tenant is not the setup tenant, grant it with an explicit role-permission row there.
