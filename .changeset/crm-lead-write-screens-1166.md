feat(crm_leads): the lead write screens (Issue #1166, ADR-0153). Five system-admin pages over the endpoints that already existed, so no API or event changed: `/admin/crm-leads/new` (enter a lead by hand through the `manual` source), `/admin/crm-leads/{id}/edit` (rename, revoke or add contact points, soft delete), `/admin/crm-leads/deleted` (restore; newest 100), `/admin/crm-leads/{id}/merge` (identity candidates with the matched kinds and masked contact points of both leads, a human-verified merge with a required note, and undo inside the window) and `/admin/crm-leads/{id}/conversion` (record a confirmed or manual conversion, reverse one). Each control is shown only when the chokepoint allows the permission of the endpoint behind it; every write carries `expectedVersion`/`If-Match` and an `Idempotency-Key`, and the conversion and intake forms send a stable `occurredAt` so a retry replays. No contact value, customer reference or evidence reference is ever returned to a page. `sql/430` adds the partial index `awcms_crm_leads_deleted_list_idx` the restore list reads. The seven `crm_leads` permissions leave the `NOT_YET_SCREENED` ledger (`crm_leads.conversions.approve`, `.leads.create`, `.leads.delete`, `.leads.restore`, `.leads.update`, `.manual_conversions.create`, `.merges.manage`); the `app` asset budget rises by the measured 6,255 B. Indonesian copy says "prospek".
