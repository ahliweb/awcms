Propose ADR-0151 (Issue #1158), Mailketing v2 and multi-account email management. Versioned v1 and v2 adapters sit behind the existing `EmailProvider` port with a staged, reversible flip; v2 is header-token only, `message_id` is provider-generated and never an idempotency key, and every documented status (200 `success:false`, 401, 402, 403, 422, 429, 5xx) has a classified outcome plus a per-connection failure budget. Each Mailketing connection becomes a `channel_account` in the #1140 registry with verified sender child accounts and an envelope-encrypted token, selected by a nullable immutable `sender_account_id` on the single `email` queue (no second queue, no sender rotation, no failover). `email` keeps the queue and suppression, `newsletter` keeps consent, `commerce` keeps customers and campaigns; no remote campaign, list or unsubscribe API is claimed. Purpose classes and consent and suppression precedence are checked at enqueue and again before dispatch. Because the provider documents no webhook signature, the callback runs in a quarantine `strict` mode that changes no customer state, with `restrictive-only` and `authenticated` modes gated on maintainer decisions. Includes twelve invariants for #1159 to #1162 and twelve open questions. Documentation only, English and Indonesian: no module, table, migration, route or connector is added.
