Narrow four over-wide scheduled-job secret grants by splitting the env resolvers that over-reached (Issue #1132, rows 1-4). `ops/awcms-jobs.env-scoped` drops 12 of its 40 grants: `blog:ads:ingest` and `blog:legacy:import` no longer receive `NEWS_MEDIA_R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `NEWS_MEDIA_R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID` or `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`; `news-media:reconcile` no longer receives the `sync-storage` pair `R2_ACCESS_KEY_ID` / `R2_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`; `blog:publish:scheduled` no longer receives `EDGE_CACHE_PURGE_TOKEN`; `analytics:purge` no longer receives `VISITOR_ANALYTICS_HASH_SALT`, which is now web-only because only the collector hashes with it. Each is done by moving the non-secret half into a file that reads no secret (`media-r2-public-config.ts`, `media-r2-separation.ts` for the check that compares the two R2 pairs, `media-reference-safety.ts`, `edge-cache/mode.ts`, `visitor-analytics-settings.ts`) and pointing the job at it; `resolveNewsMediaR2Config()` and `resolveVisitorAnalyticsConfig()` keep their exact shape and compose the narrow resolver with the secret, so every job and route that does need the secret behaves as before. `blog:publish:scheduled` keeps both R2 pairs because its managed-media readiness check reads them. `IDEMPOTENCY_HMAC_SECRET` and `DATABASE_URL` stay global pending an owner decision.

**Operator action:** replace `ops/awcms-jobs.env-global`, `ops/awcms-jobs.env-scoped` and `ops/run-job.sh` on the host together with this release's copies (the runner refuses a pair with different fingerprints). The app and web environment are unchanged.
