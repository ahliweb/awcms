feat(hr_workforce): the employment context, phase 1 foundation of the hr_* family (#945, ADR-0132). New module `hr_workforce` (`experimental`, flag off, depends only on tenant_admin and profile_identity): an employment header that REFERENCES a profile_identity party by composite foreign key and copies no name, contact or national id (and has no photo, biometric or device field), effective-dated append-only terms (office, manager, title, type, status) with no overlap, a bounded acyclic manager chain and a terminal `terminated`, tenant settings, one active employment per profile and per login enforced by the database, and a profile-merge guard. An employee reads their own employment through an ownership grant on the persisted login link (ADR-0063), via a new optional `ownershipGrant` on `defineTenantRoute`; the link itself is a high-risk, audited `assign`. Migrations `sql/176`-`sql/177` (schema, permissions); existing tenants need `identity-access:permissions:backfill`. Registered with data_lifecycle and subject-data (employments exported and retained under the employer's obligations; terms retained, not exported); the module has no admin screen yet.
