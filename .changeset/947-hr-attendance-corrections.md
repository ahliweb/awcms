hr_workforce: attendance events and corrections with approval (Issue 947, ADR-0132, migrations sql/198-sql/199). Attendance events are append-only (no UPDATE or DELETE for the runtime role, plus triggers), recorded by the database clock, with a claimed time bounded by the tenant clock-skew setting: a future claim is refused for every source, a self or supervisor event cannot be back-dated, and a kiosk or import replay is accepted up to seven days and flagged late; replay-safe by a client event key. There is no location, photo, device or network column (geolocation evidence stays a separate follow-up). A correction never edits an event: it proposes an add or a replace with a mandatory reason and starts a workflow_approval instance (fail-closed when none is published), only its status moves and that status must follow the workflow verdict, and on approval a new event supersedes the old one. The requester can never be the approver: a CHECK, a trigger over the workflow decision rows (including a delegate acting on the requester's behalf), the chokepoint self-approval guard and a handler check for a workflow that finished without a human decision. An employee reads their own events and corrections and a supervisor their reports through ownership grants on the read routes only (the manager chain, ten levels, a soft-deleted employment breaks it); the correction reason is sensitive and never reaches an audit row, event, log, write response or the workflow store. Seven new permissions (clock, attendance and corrections); existing tenants need the permission backfill, and an operator must publish a workflow under hr.attendance_correction before corrections can be requested.
