feat(booking): booking consults the hr_workforce `StaffAvailabilityPort` when a resource requires staff (#949, epic ahliweb/awcms-one#280 item 20, Booking half). New optional capability `staff_availability` (provided by `hr_workforce`, consumed by `booking` as a soft edge: lazy import in `booking/module.ts` only, no `dependencies` entry, no cycle; contract version 1.0.0 in the registry and the family manifest). Migration 305: `awcms_booking_resources.required_staff_refs` (the opaque people who serve a resource; empty means no staff constraint), `awcms_booking_staff_assignments` (one live person per claimed item and resource, a single exclusion constraint on tenant, staff reference and range refuses two live overlapping assignments of one person), a release trigger so cancel, expiry and reschedule free the person with the allocation, and the lifecycle count branch. Availability narrows to slots inside a rostered shift and not already serving a claim (`staffAvailability: "checked" | "unchecked"` on the slot answer, OpenAPI updated); a claim wins a person in the same savepoint as the units, and a staff conflict is `SLOT_UNAVAILABLE` with nothing allocated partially. With `hr_workforce` off for the tenant, or unloadable, no staff constraint applies at all. `PlaceHoldContext.staff` is required (no default) and `createBookingPort(resolveStaff)` / `createComposedBookingPort()` make the BookingPort adapter staff-aware; occupancy does not depend on the reaper (elapsed holds are ignored by the busy read and reclaimed by staff reference on claim). Reservations made while `hr_workforce` was off are not protected after enabling it (see booking.md section 10.3). Not yet: an admin surface for `required_staff_refs`.
