security(access): the ABAC policy cache is now invalidated across processes and replicas (Issue #1482). Before, `policy-cache.ts` was invalidated only by `invalidatePolicyCache` in the process that served the write, so with more than one process a new `deny` or a disabled `allow` kept its old effect elsewhere until restart — a stale allow on the authorization chokepoint. `sql/432` adds `awcms_abac_policy_versions` (one row per tenant, FORCE RLS, `awcms_app` SELECT only) and a `SECURITY DEFINER` trigger (owned by the new NOLOGIN, memberless `awcms_abac_policy_version_bump`, `search_path` pinned) that writes a fresh value from a standalone sequence into it in the same transaction as every INSERT, UPDATE, DELETE or TRUNCATE on `awcms_abac_policies`, whichever surface, migration or operator makes it. The chokepoint reads the version on the principal query it already runs (a LEFT JOIN, no extra round trip: 9 queries per authorization on a cache hit, 10 on a miss, unchanged from before) and serves a cached snapshot only when the versions are equal; callers without a principal (`/access/evaluate`, `/access/policies/simulate`, field access) pay one indexed read. `invalidatePolicyCache` stays as a local optimisation. `security:readiness` gains a critical check pinning the function body, both trigger shapes and the grants exactly, plus a rolled-back probe that every write kind moves the version and that `awcms_app` is refused a direct write. No API or event change.
