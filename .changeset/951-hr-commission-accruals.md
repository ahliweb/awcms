feat(hr_commission): the commission accrual ledger, its transitions and the sale-attribution port (#951, ADR-0132). `hr_commission` (still `experimental`) gains `awcms_hr_commission_accruals` — one commission per (source, employment), with monetary facts immutable by trigger and a status that advances `pending` → `approved` → `payable` → `paid` or to `voided` from pending or approved (a voided accrual can never be paid, also when the application is bypassed) — and `awcms_hr_commission_transitions`, an append-only log written only by the accrual's own triggers so status and log cannot diverge, with a reconciliation that proves it. A downstream adapter reports a sale through the new neutral `CommissionAccrualPort` (`_shared/ports/commission-accrual-port.ts`; the module imports nothing from commerce) or `POST /api/v1/hr-commission/accruals`: a double delivery is one accrual by a database unique key (ten racing deliveries tested), a redelivery returns the original, a different amount for the same source is 409 `SOURCE_CONFLICT`, and the amount is computed by exact `bigint` decimal arithmetic from the published rule in force on the event's own date — the body can never assert an amount, a rule version or a status. The rule and employment are validated under `FOR SHARE` by the service and by trigger (concurrency tests fail without the locks); segregation of duties is enforced in the handler and by trigger (the beneficiary can approve, release or pay none of their own accrual, the creator cannot approve it, the approver cannot pay it), the database recomputes the stored amount from the rule version and refuses a mismatch, and an event may be at most 730 days old. New permissions `accruals.read|accrue|approve|release|cancel|pay` (`accrue` and `pay` are new high-risk access actions; approve, release, cancel and pay are high-risk, idempotency-keyed and audited without any amount, base, rate, source or payment reference). Decided here: publishing a rule version whose window starts before today (UTC) now needs a `backdatedReason` (409 `RULE_BACKDATED_REASON_REQUIRED`), stamped by the database and audited as a flag only. Employee commission only (not affiliate); reversal and events are later phases; the payroll claim of an accrual (PS-19) is designed for and left to #962. Migrations `sql/187`-`sql/189` (rules back-dating, accrual schema, permissions); existing tenants need `identity-access:permissions:backfill`. Retention is answered by BOUNDED_BY_DESIGN entries (retained for the life of the tenant, no period asserted) and subject-data (export gap tracked in #985); a void permanently consumes its source key until reversal (known gap); no admin screen yet.
