security(hr_payroll): the payroll catalog check moves from deny-list scanning to allow-lists (Issue #1436, ADR-0173 Accepted 2026-11-24; owner decisions K3, Q1 = A, Q2 = A). Three rules in `payroll:catalog-boundary:check` and its daily job. (1) A non-family `SECURITY DEFINER` function, extension members included, whose owner can reach payroll (owner of a payroll object, the migration-ledger owner, an expected owner, a payroll grantee, an EXECUTE grantee of a family definer function, `pg_read_all_data`/`pg_write_all_data`/`pg_maintain`/`pg_execute_server_program`/`pg_read_server_files`/`pg_write_server_files`, a superuser, `BYPASSRLS` or `REPLICATION` role, or a member of one) is a finding (`security-definer-not-allow-listed`) unless it is an entry of the new `PAYROLL_SECURITY_DEFINER_ALLOWLIST`, keyed `schema.name(identity args)` and pinned to its owner, the digest of its whole definition (body, `SET` clauses, language, kind, volatility, strictness, leakproofness) and its EXECUTE grantees (`security-definer-allowlist-drift`, stale entries fail). The list is empty: every definer function in `sql/` is owned by a dedicated role without payroll privilege. An entry is added in a PR approved by one maintainer under the security review, naming the issue. (2) SQL-standard bodies (`BEGIN ATOMIC`, `RETURN expr`) are read from `pg_get_function_sqlbody` and their `pg_depend` calls count for the family-call and chain rules; an empty one is `function-body-unreadable`. (3) The required payroll tables are pinned by `PAYROLL_REQUIRED_TABLE_FINGERPRINTS`: every column and type; each required trigger with its function, `tgtype`, the definition digest of its trigger function, and no `WHEN`, `UPDATE OF`, arguments, deferral or constraint trigger; each foreign key with its columns, referenced columns and actions, enforced and validated. A difference is `payroll-fingerprint-drift`; a non-family relation that lacks at most 2 of the ledger's column types (names ignored, extra columns not counted, domains as their base type) is `payroll-fingerprint-match`. The static layer also refuses `CREATE OR REPLACE TRIGGER` on a payroll table or a required trigger. `PAYROLL_CATALOG_EXEMPTIONS` does not silence the new codes. A migration that adds a `SECURITY DEFINER` function owned by the migration role, or changes a required payroll table or one of its trigger functions without updating its fingerprint, now fails CI. No migration, API or event change.
