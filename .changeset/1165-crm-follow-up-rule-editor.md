feat(crm_follow_up): rule editor and simulation form on `/admin/crm-follow-up/rules` (Issue #1165, ADR-0159). With `rules.configure` the screen now creates a rule with its first revision, publishes the next immutable revision from a form seeded with the current one (carrying the `expectedVersion` the person saw, so a rule changed meanwhile answers a conflict instead of being overwritten), and archives (confirmed, irreversible). Every write sends one `Idempotency-Key` per distinct body and its body holds no clock reading, so a retry replays instead of raising `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. With `rules.read` alone a person can use the simulation: it evaluates the draft in the form against a sample event, writes nothing, and works while the tenant switch is off. Refused fields are named by their own control in the page's language, never by a server sentence. A fixed assignee and escalation recipient are typed as the person's id until a staff picker exists (README, recorded follow-up). No API, schema or permission change; no new permission was claimed or removed from the screen ledger. 60 new msgids (en/id), pure form model `src/lib/ui/crm-follow-up-rule-form.ts` with unit tests, a handler-level integration test of the form's own bodies, and four page-contract tests (each proven red under a temporary mutation of its control).
