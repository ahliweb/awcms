ops(backup): a synthetic backup-restore drill for the sensitive domains that exist today (Issue #968, generic half; owner decision K13). `bun run ops:restore-drill` migrates and seeds a disposable database (booking reservations and allocations through the real service, hr_workforce attendance), pins a snapshot, writes more data after it, dumps that snapshot with `pg_dump`, provisions the cluster-scoped `awcms_*` roles in a second cluster, restores, and verifies: row counts and per-table checksums, sequences, the point in time (no post-snapshot write is present), the booking reconciliation (booking.md section 7.1), RLS FORCE and policies and role grants compared with the source, and behaviour probes run AS `awcms_app` (isolation, refused cross-tenant write, append-only grant, not a superuser). It reports the measured RTO by phase and refuses non-loopback hosts and any database not named `awcms_drill_*`. Tests: comparators fail on deliberately broken input, a drill missing any required check fails, and a real restore is tampered with twelve ways, each turning the matching check red. `docs/awcms/restore-drill.md` (en/id) holds the LOCAL drill log with real figures, the RPO stated as a backup-policy property (no production figure invented), and the operator runbook for the production drill. The payroll half (payout accounts and key-ring retention, #957, blocked by the DPIA #976) is listed as `deferred` in every report with its hook; the issue stays open. No schema change.
