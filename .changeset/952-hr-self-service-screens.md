feat(hr_workforce, hr_commission): employee self-service, supervisor and HR screens (#952, with the employee-facing screen of #1083). Five screens under `/admin/hr/`: `my-work` (own published shifts, own effective attendance, own correction requests and the request form), `my-metrics` (own workforce facts with the definition of each figure and the way to challenge one), `my-commission` (own accruals, read-only), `team` (direct reports' facts, attendance and pending corrections) and `workforce` (HR: employments, pending corrections, recent attendance, each panel behind its own permission). Every read runs in basis `mine`/`reports` computed in the query from the persisted login link and the manager link; no screen takes a person or employment parameter, exports, downloads, shows a correction reason, or reads HR's per-person `all`-mode metrics (left to #1083). New API: `GET /api/v1/hr-workforce/me` and `GET /api/v1/hr-commission/my-accruals[/{id}]` (another employee's accrual is 404, the same as a missing id; no approver, payer or payment reference). Migration `313` seeds `hr_workforce.self_service.read` and `hr_commission.my_accruals.read` (screen gates; nothing grants them to a role, run `identity-access:permissions:backfill` for existing tenants). `hr_workforce` and `hr_commission` are now `active`. Security review fixes: every panel is authorised with its route's guard and ownership grant (an ABAC deny hides it), the supervisor and HR loads record the same `sensitive_read` audit rows as the routes (fail-closed), the supervisor screen reads direct reports only since each line began, and the own-only commission readers are on the payroll read-import registry.
