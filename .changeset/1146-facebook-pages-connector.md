feat(social_publishing): Facebook Pages connector, off by default, and the dispatcher's own database role for the whole job (Issue #1146, ADR-0157 section 6). **Nothing publishes and no social token is decrypted unless `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_FACEBOOK_ENABLED=true` (with `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=true`)**: the registry has no Facebook adapter and the dispatcher is composed with a credential port that refuses every request. The connector posts text and link (`POST /{page-id}/feed`) and JPEG/PNG photos up to 4 MB (`POST /{page-id}/photos`) through Graph API v27.0 with the Page's own access token as a bearer header, through `ssrfSafeFetch` with no redirects; WebP, larger images and video are refused at enqueue, never downgraded. Graph errors are classified: an expired or revoked token or a withdrawn permission fails the job `NEEDS_REAUTH` and degrades the account (new `ChannelPublisherHealthPort`, capability `channel_account` 1.2.0), so its other jobs are held; throttling (4, 17, 32, 341, 613, 80001) waits what `X-Business-Use-Case-Usage` asks; a lost answer, a gateway 5xx, Graph code 1 and a duplicate post (506) become `needs_reconciliation`, never a blind retry (the Graph API has no idempotency key). **Operator action when enabling the module:** `social-publishing:dispatch` now runs ONLY as `awcms_social_dispatch` through the new job-scoped `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_DATABASE_URL` (activate LOGIN on the role; no fallback, and `config:validate` requires it when `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED=true` and refuses a login shared with the app or worker); it holds `CHANNEL_SECRETS_ENCRYPTION_KEYS`, and `ops/run-job.sh` withholds `DATABASE_URL`, `WORKER_DATABASE_URL` and `SETUP_DATABASE_URL` from it (new dedicated-role job class in `scripts/jobs-env-allowlist.ts`); it refuses to start with a shared login present or when `current_user` is not that role. Migration `sql/373` grants the role exactly the dispatcher's columns and bounds every write with RESTRICTIVE policies (no re-queue of a failed or unknown job, no cancel, only `connected` to `degraded` of a live social account, only this module's audit rows and events), pinned exactly and probed by `security:readiness`. `awcms_worker` and `whatsapp:dispatch` are unchanged. Reply, comments, DM and inbound for Facebook are not in this change.
