Add the `social_publishing` module: account-scoped social publishing behind the existing `SocialPublishingPort`, as a foundation with NO provider connector (Issue #1145, ADR-0157, proposed; ADR-0148 capability C3). One publication carries an immutable content snapshot (text, an `https` link and a media object verified through `media_library` for type, size and a known rights status) and fans out to ONE JOB PER TARGET `channel_account`, idempotent per article, trigger and account by a unique key, announced by one domain event; a job names the account and holds no token and no secret reference, because the dispatcher resolves the credential for that account at dispatch time. A tenant can require approval (the default; the creator of a manual publication cannot approve it) and schedule a publication; an operator can approve, cancel, reschedule and retry ONE failed job. Provider adapters are plug-ins behind a `SocialPublishAdapter` interface whose per-provider capability map is a ceiling and never a grant: content it cannot carry is refused, never downgraded or truncated, and an account still needs a connected status, an active connection and a granted `publish` capability with evidence, re-checked at enqueue and again at dispatch (revoked or disabled fails the job, degraded or an expired grant holds it, nothing is rerouted). `bun run social-publishing:dispatch` claims with a lease, enforces fair share and a per-account daily cap under an advisory lock, calls the provider outside any transaction through an `ssrfSafeFetch` client that follows no redirect and is cancelled (not abandoned) by the call's timeout, with a per-account circuit breaker, and finalises with a compare-and-set; an unknown outcome (a timeout, a throw after the request was sent) is retried automatically only for an adapter whose provider dedupes on the idempotency key, otherwise the job is `needs_reconciliation` and waits for an operator (so is a job whose lease expired while in flight, for such a provider: never re-sent by the dispatcher); a published job is terminal (database trigger), so a partial failure never replays a sibling and never touches the source article. Migrations `sql/330` to `sql/331` (FORCE RLS, integrity triggers including approval and maker/checker rules enforced by the database, nine permissions, and a read of the module-enablement row for the worker role), thirteen operations under `/api/v1/social-publishing`, four `awcms.social_publishing.*` events, capability `channel_account` 1.0.0 to 1.1.0 (two new exported ports, additive) and `social_publishing` 1.0.0. The composition roots (`POST /api/v1/blog/posts/{id}/publish` and `blog:publish:scheduled`) swap `blog_content`'s no-op adapter for the real one only when `SOCIAL_PUBLISHING_ENABLED` is on; a deployment that configures nothing behaves exactly as before, and with the module on nothing is published until a connector (#1146 to #1151) registers an adapter and brings the credential path: the worker role's secret visibility is NOT widened (`sql/328` stays WhatsApp-only) and the dispatch job holds no key ring, so social tokens become readable only under a separate DB role the first connector adds (ADR-0157 section 6). A tenant that switched the module off is neither enqueued for nor dispatched. `ssrfSafeFetch` gains an optional `signal` (no behaviour change when absent). No admin screen yet, no retention sweep, no caption templates (see `docs/awcms/social-publishing.md`).
