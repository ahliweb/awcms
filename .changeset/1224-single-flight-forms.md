Single flight (double-submit protection) is now the default for every form in the CMS, admin and public (Issue #1224). `onSubmit`, `onSubmitAll` and `onAction` in `src/lib/ui/admin-form-client.ts` own an in-flight flag per form (per button for `onAction`): while the handler's promise is pending a further `submit` or `click` is ignored, not queued, and the native POST is still cancelled so Enter and implicit submission cannot fall through; the flag is released in `finally`, so a throw, an `{ ok: false }` and a network error all leave the form usable. While pending the form shows `aria-busy="true"` and its submit button is disabled without changing its label; `lockElement` is now idempotent over one shared registry, so a page that also locks its button keeps its own busy label and release restores exactly what was there. A handler that navigates on success keeps the form locked until the page leaves (`mutateAndReload`, the new `holdForNavigation()`, or a `lockElement` the page leaves held) and a bfcache back button (`pageshow` with `persisted`) restores it. The pure state machine is `createSingleFlight` (injected ports, no new dependency). The audit of all 216 entry points found 197 protected by `lockElement`/`mutateAndReload`, 11 by a hand-rolled flag and 4 by nothing (`.account-sso-link`, the preview overlay's Enter-to-save, and the two reason-panel reversal forms that called `mutateAndReload(null, …)`); all are now covered. The five public auth forms (login, register, forgot/reset password, accept invitation), the language switcher, the media upload and rights forms, the push-notification buttons and the repository-progress clear button were moved from direct listeners onto the helpers, and the preview overlay got its own `saving` flag. Behaviour for a user: a fast double tap now sends one request instead of two. No endpoint, schema or permission changed. New: `docs/awcms/ui-single-flight-audit.md` (EN + ID, generated by `bun run ui:single-flight:audit --write`), a source-contract test that fails when a page binds `submit` or a mutating `click` outside the helpers, unit tests, and Playwright specs for a double click on the offices create form and on login (written; run in CI `e2e-smoke`). After review: a throw always releases the lock even if the handler had locked its button, the navigation hold is one-shot and is released after 10 s when the page is still alive, and a release no longer overwrites a `disabled` the page changed during the request. The client asset budget is raised by 74 B (`APP_BUDGET_BYTES` 346,353 -> 346,427, ledgered in `scripts/client-asset-budget.ts`); the first pass was a net of -52 B.
